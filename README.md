# Arrakis

**Your whole desktop, in one small app.**

A dock, desktop widgets, a wallpaper that follows the sun and the weather, a clipboard with snippets,
a screenshot editor, a focus timer and battery health, plus a command bar to reach all of it, for Windows 10 and 11.
Turn on only the parts you want.

## What's inside

Arrakis is one app with seven modules. Each one has its own switch, so it only does what you ask it to.

| Module | What it does | Shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| **Dock** | A floating dock for your apps, with window previews, folder stacks and a dock on every screen. Can replace the taskbar. | Always on |
| **Widgets** | 15 widgets, from clocks, weather and your calendar to to-dos, stocks, headlines and a photo frame. Saved layouts and meeting alerts. | Ctrl+Alt+W |
| **Wallpaper** | Scenes that follow the real sun and the weather outside, your own photos, or Bing's photo of the day. On every screen. | Automatic |
| **Clipboard** | Everything you copy, searchable. Snippets with shortcodes, paste as UPPERCASE and more, collections, encrypted sync between PCs. | Ctrl+Alt+V |
| **Capture** | A full screenshot tool: area, window, full screen, all screens or delayed. Edit with 14 tools, effects and frames, then copy, save, print or pin. | Ctrl+Alt+S |
| **Focus** | A timer pill that silences notifications, keeps distracting apps away, can start from your calendar and gives you a weekly report. | Ctrl+Alt+F |
| **Battery** | Battery wear, charge history, tips on your charging habits, low-battery alerts, power modes and a report you can print. | Laptops |

And across the whole app: a **command bar** on **Alt+Space**, **light and dark themes** with six accent colours,
**automatic updates**, and **backup** to move everything to a new PC.

## A quick tour

### The hub

Every module in one window, each with its own switch. Click a card for its settings.

<img src="docs/images/hub.png" alt="The Arrakis hub with a card and a switch for each module" width="900">

### Command bar

Press **Alt+Space** anywhere. Type to find apps, files from your usual folders, Windows settings pages and things
you've copied. Type a sum like `999 * 1.25` and press Enter to copy the answer. It can also take a screenshot,
start a 25, 50 or 90 minute focus session, switch power modes, load a widget layout or search the web.
Change the shortcut, or turn it off, in Arrakis > Settings.

<img src="docs/images/commandbar.png" alt="The command bar working out a sum, with apps, actions, a file, a Windows setting and a clipboard item below" width="900">

### Light or dark

Pick Dark, Light or Match Windows, and one of six accent colours, in Arrakis > Settings.

<img src="docs/images/light.png" alt="The hub in the light theme with the appearance options" width="900">

### Dock

Your pinned apps and everything that's open. Icons magnify as you move across them.
Click to switch, click again to minimise, right-click to pin or close. What's playing and your focus timer sit
next to it. Pick from three styles, and hide the Windows taskbar if you want the dock to replace it.

- **Window previews**: rest the mouse on an open app to see live pictures of its windows. Click one to switch, or close it from there.
- **Window counts**: apps with more than one window show how many under the icon.
- **Folder stacks**: Downloads is there to start with. Click it to see the newest files, and drop any folder on the dock to add another.
- **Drag to pin**: drop an app, shortcut or file on the dock to pin it.
- **Every screen**: turn on a dock for each monitor, each showing the windows on that screen.

<img src="docs/images/dock.png" alt="The dock with live previews of two browser windows and the Downloads folder stack open" width="900">

### Widgets

Drag them anywhere. They stay behind your windows like part of the wallpaper. Right-click for size, style and options,
or press **Ctrl+Alt+W** to show remove buttons. There are 15 to choose from:

clock, weather, calendar, system rings, battery, now playing, notes, countdown, **agenda**, **to-do list**,
**quick launch**, **world clocks**, **photo frame**, **stocks** and **headlines** from any news feed.

- **Your calendar**: paste your calendar's .ics link (Google, Outlook and iCloud all have one). The calendar widget
  marks busy days, the agenda shows what's next, and you get a **heads-up 10 minutes before each meeting**.
- **Small, medium or large**, from the right-click menu.
- **Snap to grid** so everything lines up.
- **Layouts**: save where everything is as, say, Work or Gaming, and switch between them from the Widgets page or the command bar.

<img src="docs/images/widgets.png" alt="World clocks, agenda, quick launch, stocks, to-do list, headlines and a photo frame on the desktop, with a meeting alert" width="900">

### Wallpaper

Four scenes drawn at your screen's exact resolution. Arrakis works out sunrise and sunset for your city each day and
switches between them. The sun moves across the sky through the day, and clouds, rain, snow, fog and storms appear
when they're happening outside. Each screen gets a picture drawn at its own size.

Prefer your own pictures? Point it at a folder for a slideshow, or choose a photo for each of dawn, day, sunset and night.
Your original wallpaper comes back when you turn it off.

<img src="docs/images/wallpaper.png" alt="The wallpaper page with a large preview and the dawn, day, sunset and night scenes" width="900">

### Clipboard

Press **Ctrl+Alt+V** wherever you are. Type to search, Enter to paste, Shift+Enter for plain text.
Links, colours and images are recognised. Password managers that mark copies as private are never recorded.

- **Snippets**: save text you type often and give it a shortcode. Type `;addr` in any app and it becomes your address.
  `{date}`, `{time}` and `{clipboard}` are filled in for you.
- **Paste as**: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, one line, list to commas and back, sorted lines,
  duplicates removed, or links with the tracking taken off.
- **Collections**: put items into your own lists, like Work or Recipes, and filter by them.
- **Private items**: anything that looks like a card number, bank details or a password is hidden, kept out of search
  and sync, and deleted after a minute.
- **Sync between PCs**: choose a shared folder (OneDrive, Dropbox, a network drive) and a password on each PC.
  History is encrypted before it leaves your PC.

<img src="docs/images/clipboard.png" alt="The clipboard panel filtered to snippets with the Paste as menu open, beside a snippet expanding in a message" width="900">

### Capture

A complete screenshot studio, built in.

**Capture** an area (**Ctrl+Alt+S**), a window (**Ctrl+Alt+D**), the full screen (**Ctrl+Alt+A**), every screen at once,
the same area as last time, or after a 5-second countdown. While you pick, a magnifier shows the exact pixel and its colour,
windows light up as you hover them, and **Space** switches between area and window mode. You can also set it up to use
the Print Screen key, with Alt, Ctrl and Shift for the other modes.

<img src="docs/images/capture-overlay.png" alt="Picking an area, with the size shown and a magnifier showing the pixel under the mouse" width="900">

**Edit** with 14 tools: select and move, box, ellipse, line, arrow, freehand pen, text, speech-bubble callouts, numbered steps,
highlighter, pixelate, blur, crop and a colour picker. Everything can be recoloured, moved, deleted and undone.
Then rotate, flip, resize, greyscale, invert, add a border, a drop shadow or torn edges, and put it on a frame.
**Copy text** reads the words in a screenshot, and email addresses are blurred for you, all on your PC.

**Share** it: copy, quick-save with your own file name pattern, save as PNG, JPG, BMP, GIF or TIFF, print, open in Paint
or any other app, or pin it on top of everything. If you don't need the editor, set captures to go straight to the clipboard or a folder.

<img src="docs/images/capture.png" alt="The screenshot editor with 14 tools, a callout, numbered steps, an arrow, a blurred email, image effects and a sunset frame" width="900">

### Focus

Start a session and a small pill shows your time at the top of the screen. Notifications are silenced, the dock
gets out of the way and widgets dim. Rain, ocean, brown noise or white noise are made on the fly, so there's
nothing extra to download.

<img src="docs/images/focus.png" alt="A focus session with the timer ring, ambient sound choices and a chart of the last 7 days" width="900">

- **Keep distractions away**: list apps like Steam or Discord. If one opens during focus time, Arrakis gives you a nudge or minimises it.
- **What are you working on?** Add a task and it shows on the pill and is saved with the session.
- **Open on start**: a playlist, a lo-fi stream or the document you're working on opens when a session begins.
- **Start from your calendar**: events with "Focus" in the title (or any word you choose) start a session that runs until the event ends.
- **Weekly report**: this week against last, the last 30 days, your best day, your longest streak, eight weeks of history and your top tasks.

<img src="docs/images/focus-report.png" alt="The focus report with weekly totals, an eight-week chart, top tasks and blocked apps" width="900">

### Battery

How much your battery holds compared with when it was new, how many charge cycles it's done, the last week of
charge levels, and a reminder to unplug at the level you choose.

- **Tips for your battery** based on how you actually charge, like spotting nights spent at 100%.
- **Low-battery alert** at the level you pick, naming the apps using the most power right then.
- **Power modes**: switch between Battery saver, Balanced and Best performance in one click (desktops too).
- **Health report**: one page with health, capacity, cycles and a chart, to print or save as a PDF. Handy when selling a laptop.

<img src="docs/images/battery.png" alt="Battery health, tips, the three power modes and the low-battery alert" width="900">

> The pictures above are design renders of each screen, made from the same layouts the app uses. Your apps,
> weather and numbers will be your own.

## Install

1. Download **[Arrakis-Setup.exe](https://github.com/bhxuk/arrakis/releases/latest/download/Arrakis-Setup.exe)**.
2. Double-click it. You don't need admin rights; it installs just for you.
3. Windows may say **"Windows protected your PC"**, because the installer isn't code-signed yet.
   Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
4. Arrakis opens on the hub. Switch on what you want.

**Updates** install themselves: when a new version is out, Arrakis downloads it quietly and reopens (never in the
middle of a focus session). You can turn this off in Arrakis > Settings.

**New PC?** In Arrakis > Settings, choose **Export** to save everything (settings, widgets, clipboard, snippets,
focus and battery history) to one file, then **Import** it on the other PC.

It needs Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit. Everything it needs is built in, so there's
nothing else to install.

**Uninstall** from Settings > Apps, like any other app. The taskbar is put back if Arrakis had hidden it.
Your settings and clipboard history stay in `%AppData%\Arrakis` in case you come back; delete that folder to remove them.

### If the taskbar disappears and won't come back

Open Start, find **Arrakis**, and choose **Restore Windows taskbar**. You can also untick
"Hide the Windows taskbar" in Arrakis > Dock, or right-click the Arrakis icon by the clock and choose
**Show Windows taskbar**.

## Privacy

Everything Arrakis stores stays on your PC. The only things it fetches from the internet are:

- the weather, from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) (free, no account), if you set a city;
- Bing's photo of the day, only if you choose that wallpaper mode;
- your calendar, from the .ics link you give it, if you add one;
- stock prices and news headlines, only if you add those widgets;
- a check for a newer version on GitHub, which you can switch off in Settings.

Clipboard sync only writes to the folder you choose, and the file is encrypted with your password first.

## Build it yourself

You'll need the free [.NET 8 SDK](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/8.0).

```powershell
git clone https://github.com/bhxuk/arrakis
cd arrakis
dotnet publish src/Arrakis/Arrakis.csproj -c Release -r win-x64 --self-contained true -p:PublishSingleFile=true -o publish
publish\Arrakis.exe
```

To make the installer too, install [Inno Setup 6](https://jrsoftware.org/isinfo.php) and run
`iscc installer\Arrakis.iss`. Every push to GitHub builds both automatically. When the `<Version>` in
`src/Arrakis/Arrakis.csproj` changes, the build publishes a new release with `Arrakis-Setup.exe` attached.

### How it's put together

```
src/Arrakis/
  App.cs              start-up, single instance, turning modules on and off
  HubWindow.cs        the main window
  Core/               Windows calls, settings, hotkeys, tray icon, media, weather, calendar,
                      command bar, backup, UI pieces
  Modules/            Dock, Widgets, Wallpaper, Clipboard, Capture, Focus, Battery
installer/Arrakis.iss the Setup.exe recipe
docs/                 website, user guide and screenshots
```
