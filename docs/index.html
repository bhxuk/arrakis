The hub

Everything in one place

Each module is a card with its own switch. Click a card for its settings. Close the window and Arrakis carries on quietly by the clock.

  • Starts with Windows if you want it to
  • Checks GitHub for updates
  • Settings and history stay on your PC
Dock

A dock that gets out of the way

Pinned and open apps, with a dot under the ones that are running. Icons magnify as you move across them.

  • Click to switch, click again to minimise
  • Music and focus timer cards beside it
  • Floating glass, minimal pill or full-width edge bar
  • Optionally hides the Windows taskbar, and always gives it back
Widgets

Useful things, on your desktop

Widgets sit behind your windows like part of the wallpaper. Drag them anywhere and they stay put.

  • Weather from Open-Meteo, no account needed
  • Glass, Solid or Outline styles
  • Double-click a note to type
  • Ctrl+Alt+W shows remove buttons
Wallpaper

A desktop that follows the sun

Four scenes, drawn at your screen's exact resolution, that change with the real sunrise and sunset where you live.

  • Or keep one scene you love
  • Or Bing's photo of the day
  • Your old wallpaper comes back when you switch it off
Clipboard

Never lose something you copied

Press Ctrl+Alt+V anywhere. Type to search, Enter to paste.

  • Links, colours and images recognised
  • Pin the things you paste every day
  • Stack several copies and paste them in one go
  • Password managers' private copies are never saved
Capture

Screenshots that explain themselves

Press Ctrl+Alt+S, drag over what you want, then mark it up.

  • Arrows, boxes, text, highlights, blur and numbered steps
  • Sunset, ocean or meadow frames with shadow
  • Email addresses blurred automatically, on your PC
  • Copy, save, or pin it above everything
Focus

Quiet time, on a timer

Press Ctrl+Alt+F and a small pill counts down at the top of your screen.

  • Notifications silenced, dock hidden, widgets dimmed
  • Rain, ocean, brown or white noise
  • Breaks start by themselves
  • See your week and your streak
Battery

Know how your battery is really doing

Windows hides most of this in a text report. Arrakis puts it on one page.

  • Health compared with when it was new
  • Charge cycles and a 7-day chart
  • A reminder to unplug at 80%, or your own limit

Screens shown are design renders of the app's layouts. Your apps, weather and numbers will be your own.

Install

Up and running in a minute

Download

Get Arrakis-Setup.exe. It has everything it needs built in.

Run it

Double-click the file. It installs just for you, so no admin password is needed.

Switch things on

Arrakis opens on the hub. Turn on the modules you want and you're done.

Seeing "Windows protected your PC"? That appears for apps that aren't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
Good to know

Questions

Will it mess up my taskbar?

Only if you ask it to hide it, and it always puts it back when Arrakis closes. If anything goes wrong, Start > Arrakis > Restore Windows taskbar brings it straight back.

Does it slow my PC down?

Modules you switch off don't run at all. The hub shows how much memory Arrakis is using.

Which Windows versions work?

Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) and Windows 11, 64-bit.

How do I uninstall it?

Settings > Apps, like any other app. Your settings stay in %AppData%\Arrakis in case you come back; delete that folder to remove them too.

Your data stays on your PC

Clipboard history, notes, focus stats and settings are stored locally. Arrakis only goes online for:

  • the weather, from Open-Meteo, if you set a city
  • Bing's photo of the day, if you choose that wallpaper
  • checking GitHub for a newer version, which you can switch off
